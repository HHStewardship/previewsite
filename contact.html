Before you call

Things worth having to hand

None of it is required, and none of it needs to be exact. It just makes the first thirty minutes count for more.

  1. How often you are there

    Weekends, seasons, or the occasional week. The visit rhythm is built around it.

  2. What already worries you

    The corner that leaks, the crawl space, the vendor who never quite finishes.

  3. Who currently has access

    Cleaners, neighbours, family, a lockbox. We work with what already exists.

  4. Whether it is ever rented

    Short-term rental changes the turnover rhythm and the readiness standard.
